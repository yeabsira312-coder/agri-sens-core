# Research Overview

## Objective
Investigate how short‑term thermal spikes and shifts in precipitation patterns affect crop yield stability and seasonal farming cycles.

## Methodology
- **Baseline Climate Anomaly Modeling** – Establish historical climate normals and detect deviations in temperature and precipitation.
- **Vegetation Index Analysis** – Compute NDVI and related indices from satellite imagery to quantify vegetation stress.
- **Correlation Assessment** – Relate temperature and precipitation anomalies to changes in NDVI and documented yield outcomes.

## Key Indicators
- **Temperature Variance** – Frequency and magnitude of localized heat spikes.
- **Precipitation Shift** – Changes in total seasonal rainfall and timing.
- **Soil Moisture Proxy** – Derived from combined temperature‑precipitation data.
- **Yield Impact** – Comparative analysis of observed yields versus anomaly metrics.

The analysis aims to identify high‑vulnerability agricultural zones and provide early warning signals for drought or heat stress conditions.
